QUICK GUIDE

How to use EventFlow

As a participant

  1. Create a Participant account.
  2. Browse and open an event.
  3. Register to receive your ticket.
  4. Check your dashboard for reminders.
  5. Use your ticket code at check-in.

As an organizer

  1. Create an Organizer account.
  2. Create an event from your dashboard.
  3. Add the event and reminder details.
  4. View participants and ticket sales.
  5. Use ticket codes to check people in.
